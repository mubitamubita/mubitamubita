My Story

Growing up in the vibrant heart of Lusaka, I was always drawn to stories—those whispered under the stars and the ones hidden in the pages of old books. My love for storytelling began as a child, scribbling tales in notebooks and imagining worlds beyond my own. Today, I write to explore the shadows of the human mind, crafting stories that challenge, thrill, and inspire. This is my journey—a fusion of passion, curiosity, and an unyielding desire to leave a mark through words.
